# Trust Center

Welcome to the Palo Alto Networks Trust Center. Security, privacy, and compliance are core to our mission of preventing successful cyberattacks.

## Security

Our platform is built on a foundation of robust security practices. We continuously monitor and improve our defenses to ensure the highest level of protection for our customers.

- **Encryption**: Data is encrypted at rest and in transit.
- **Access Control**: Strict access controls and regular audits.
- **Incident Response**: A dedicated incident response team available 24/7.

## Compliance

We comply with major international and regional standards, ensuring our solutions meet the rigorous demands of global enterprises.

- SOC 2 Type II
- ISO 27001
- GDPR & CCPA readiness

## Privacy

We respect your privacy and are dedicated to transparent data practices. For more details, please review our [Privacy Policy](/legal-notices/privacy).
